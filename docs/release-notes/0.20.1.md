- **New mail shows up on its own.** Brain no longer checks for mail on a timer from the open tab: the mail service tells the tab the moment it sees a change, and the list, the open letter and the notification bell follow within a second. A letter you already scrolled past stays where you left it.
- **Recent letters open instantly.** The bodies of your newest 200 letters from the last 30 days are fetched ahead of time, up to 48 MB per account, so opening one paints at once instead of waiting on Gmail or your mail server. Opening a letter always goes first. Remote images still load only when you open a letter.
- Turning Mail off stops the ahead-of-time fetching too.
