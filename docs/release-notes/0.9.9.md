- Smart sort opens the moment you press it. The pages it is about to sort sit there while Brain thinks, so you can see what it is working on and change your mind, and when the answer comes back they move into the sections it chose and into the two columns it is about to write. If the save fails the dialog now stays open and tells you, instead of closing without a word.
- On the phone, Search and Pages are no longer panels that slide over the app. Each one takes the whole screen the way Mail does, arrives the same way, and leaves with the usual back swipe, so neither needs its own Back button any more. Home now stays lit while you are reading a page, and pressing it takes you back to the page you were writing.
- On the dark theme, the pale edge along a selected row is much quieter. It was the brightest thing on the screen and it made every selected row look lit up.
- You can connect up to seven mail accounts. With that many, a full round of checking every account takes longer, so mail in the last account in the queue arrives a little later than it used to.
- Covers appear faster. Brain starts fetching a page's cover the moment you open the page rather than waiting for the page itself to load first, and your browser now keeps the picture instead of downloading it again on every visit.
- Settings has a Donate section. Brain is free and I run it myself, and donations go into the work on it.
