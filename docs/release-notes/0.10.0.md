- Brain has Tasks. They live under Mail in the sidebar and in a sixth tab on the phone, with Today, Upcoming, Someday, an inbox for anything undated, a Logbook of what you finished, and a view for each category you make up, like Family or Work. Things is the reference, on purpose: quiet rows, one gesture to complete, a hold before the row leaves, and an Undo while it is leaving.
- A checkbox in any note can become a task. Hover a `- [ ]` line and a small word appears; press it and the task exists, with a date, a deadline, a category and a repeat that live in Tasks while the text and the tick stay in the note. Tick it in either place and the other follows. Delete the line and the task is not lost: it reads "line removed from ‹page›" and keeps its history.
- Checkboxes in notes are now real. They were drawn as plain bullets; now they draw a check when you press them, on the phone as well.
- Repeats keep one open instance. Complete it and the next one appears, dated from the schedule rather than from today, so a week you missed does not pile up. Every completion stays in the Logbook.
- Home is one column now: the tasks that matter today, the latest mail across your accounts, and the last pages that changed. The capture field at the top can make a task as well as a page.
- The sidebar row "Today thoughts" is now "Journal", so Today means one thing.
- Two people in the same note used to conflict on every tick. Now a tick from one side merges into the other side's save as long as the other side did not touch that line. A moved or edited line still conflicts, on purpose, because that is the only safe answer.
- Tasks are files, like everything else: `_tasks/` inside your notes folder, one Markdown file per task with the record in its frontmatter. Git sees them, backups carry them, and MCP can read them with `list_tasks` and `get_task`.
- Portable archives are format 2 and carry every task, body and repeat log included. A format 1 archive from an older Brain imports unchanged; a format 2 archive does not open in an older Brain.
- A task body that starts with `---` is written as it is. The same slip still exists for a page body that starts with `---`; that one is tracked separately and comes next.
- On a 320px-wide phone the six tabs sit closer than Brain's own 20px rule allows; the labels do not collide. Wider phones are unaffected.
- Three things I know about and did not finish for this release: a task line cut from one note and pasted into another detaches instead of following (re-link it from Tasks); in Upcoming, days past next week each stand as their own group rather than folding into months; and on a desktop the deadline chip and the note's task popover still open the browser's own date picker, where the reschedule control already has Brain's list. All three come next.
