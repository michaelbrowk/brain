- An archived letter can go back to the Inbox. Open it in All Mail, Starred or Sent and choose Move to Inbox. Undo takes it back out. In All Mail and Starred the button follows the letter: Archive while it is in the Inbox, Move to Inbox when it is not, and `e` does whichever is shown.
- Letters built from broken table markup keep the layout their sender meant. Brain now cleans a letter from the same tree a browser builds, so a table that closed in the wrong place no longer spills its cells down the page. Letters already cached are cleaned again the next time you open them.
- All inboxes keeps loading older letters as you scroll after a sync pause, after Try again, and after Done empties a section. It used to stop until the page was reloaded.
- A notification or a ⌘K pick opens the letter from the mailbox it named, even when another folder is on screen.
- Going back to a page while the previous one is still sliding away puts your typing into the page you arrived at.
- The `/` and `[[` menus take Enter the moment they appear, leave keys typed into ⌘K or a dialog alone, and let an input method finish its word.
- ⌘K keeps focus when it opens over Home, and search highlights stay on the right letters in words such as "İstanbul".
