- A day in the When calendar picks that day and leaves the panel standing. Pressing one used to shut the calendar with nothing saved. The panel is drawn outside the row it belongs to, and the row read a press inside it as a second press on itself and folded, which took the picker away before the day it was holding had gone anywhere. Done, a press outside and the sheet's grip still save and close, and Escape still throws the day away.
- A chip in an expanded task row keeps its whole shape. The second line of the row clips while it grows from nothing, and the clip stood flush against the chips, so the focus ring on one came back as two slivers down its left and right edges with the top and the bottom cut off. It stands five pixels clear of them now, which is what a ring reaches, and the row is the height it always was.
- An expanded task row folds when you press somewhere else, and the grey capsule goes with it. The row used to hold its chips through a press on the page below the list, on another row or on the header, and the only ways out were Escape and a second press on the row itself. The capsule stayed behind even then, which reads as a task still holding the focus it was asked to give up. It is drawn while the Tasks column has the keyboard and not otherwise, and the place it marks is kept, so a Tab back into the column finds it on the same row. Moving the keyboard off the row folds it the same way. A press inside the calendar, the repeat menu or the category list is not somewhere else: that press closes the panel, and the row folds on the one after it.
