- An outgoing message carries 10 MiB of files, up from 8. The mail service runs under 232 MiB of memory (296 hard) instead of 192 (256), and the cap was measured against the new bar with the shipped probe: at 10 MiB the enqueue peaks at 192 MiB and a drain of twenty or a hundred queued messages at 197, twenty MiB under the bar; 11 MiB clears it by seven and is recorded, not taken. The derived caps follow: 17 MiB for the finished message, 24 MiB for a send or draft body; the IMAP relay stays at 1 MiB and nginx does not move. The unit file and the constant the probe judges by are pinned together now, so the two cannot drift apart again.
