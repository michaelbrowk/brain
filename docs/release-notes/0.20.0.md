- **New senders.** The first letter from someone you have never written to or heard from waits in its own section at the top of the Inbox, with Block and Accept on the row. Accept moves it to People and lets that sender straight in from then on. Block archives their mail now and on arrival, and lists them under Settings › Mail › Blocked senders. Both come with Undo, and the reader offers the same choice for everyone at a domain. Everyone you already corresponded with is known the moment the screen turns on, replies in a conversation are never held, and a stranger's first letter sends no notification. Turn it off in Settings › Mail.
- **Attach files to a new letter.** Use the paperclip or drop files anywhere on the sheet: up to 10 files and 10 MB per letter. A file that does not fit is named in the message line. Files go out with the letter and never sit in Drafts.
- Switching From on the sheet keeps what you wrote, your files and your place.
- **New mail arrives sooner.** Gmail is checked every 20 seconds instead of every minute. An IMAP account holds an IDLE connection, so the server tells Brain about a new letter within seconds. Opening a letter no longer triggers an extra check with the provider.
