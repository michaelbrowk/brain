- A visitor you let edit a shared page can now make subpages inside it. The name is asked once, at the moment the page is created, because editing through a link cannot rename a page afterwards.
- The Hub says who edited a shared page when that page is the only one you have shared. It used to stay silent in exactly that case, which is the one most people are in.
- On a phone, a row no longer keeps a highlight after you tap it, and the search list no longer opens with its first row already lit. Both were desktop behaviour arriving on a touch screen, where there is no pointer to move away.
- The soft colour on the background is quieter on the desktop and gone on the phone. On the dark theme it was as bright as a row under the cursor, and on a phone it covered the whole screen instead of a corner.
