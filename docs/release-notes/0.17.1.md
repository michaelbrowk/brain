- Discarding a letter no longer asks a question. The trash takes the sheet down at once and a pill says "Draft discarded" with Undo and a nine-second ring; the draft is deleted only when the ring runs out, the tab hides, or another letter is opened. Undo brings the same draft back with its text. A letter with nothing in it leaves without a pill.
- From is a switch when two or more accounts can send: a quiet menu on the From row; choosing another account carries the addresses, the subject and the body over and leaves no orphan in Drafts. Arrow keys walk the menu and Enter chooses. Replies and forwards keep From as text.
- Smaller things on the sheet: the phone's tab bar slides away instead of vanishing, resumed Cc and Bcc rows join the entrance, inputs stay at 16 px on a touch screen even at tablet width, "Cc Bcc" is quiet text, and on the phone a refused send gets its own line instead of truncating.
- Under the hood, two copies of the library that decides which layer answers Escape lived side by side; there is one now, so Escape inside a menu closes the menu and nothing else.
